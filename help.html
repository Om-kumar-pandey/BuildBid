Frequently Asked Questions
Find quick answers to common questions
How do I create a BuildBid account? ▼
Click on the Sign Up option and provide your required account information. After completing verification, you can access your contractor dashboard.
How can I post a new project? ▼
Go to the Projects section or Post Requirement section from the sidebar. Enter the project details, requirements, dates and other information and submit the project.
How does the bidding process work? ▼
Contractors can review available projects and submit their bids. You can compare bids and select the contractor that best matches your requirements.
How do I withdraw my earnings? ▼
Go to Earnings and select Withdraw Money. Your available balance can be transferred to your registered bank account according to the applicable payment process.
How can I track my material order? ▼
Open My Orders to view your orders. Select an order and use Track Material to view the current delivery status and tracking timeline.
How can I get my contractor profile verified? ▼
Open Get Verified from the sidebar. Complete your profile and upload the required documents. Once submitted, the verification team can review your information.
How do I add a team member? ▼
Open My Team and select Add Team Member. Enter the member's details, role, rate and project assignment and save the information.
How can I contact BuildBid support? ▼
You can create a support ticket using the Create Support Ticket option or contact the available support channels shown on this page.
