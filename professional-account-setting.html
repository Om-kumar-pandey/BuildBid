Take a break from client requests without losing your reputation, ratings, or verified documents.

  • Hides your public card and published services from client discovery and direct hire searches.
  • Preserves all client reviews, verified credentials, and historical project proposals safely.

Permanently Delete Account

Permanently terminate your BuildBid professional account, remove personal data, and revoke credentials.

  • Deletes profile picture, deactivates published services, and scrambles login credentials permanently.
  • Immediately invalidates active JWT sessions across all devices.
  • Shared project applications and consultation contracts are preserved for legal and audit compliance.
  • Security Prerequisite: Requires verification of BOTH your primary email and mobile number via secure OTP.
All deactivation and deletion operations are backed by server-authoritative state transitions and cryptographic audit logging.
