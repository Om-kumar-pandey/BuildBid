BuildBid Security & Uniqueness Policy

  • One Email = One Account: Each normalized email address is strictly bound to exactly one BuildBid account across all roles (Customer, Contractor, Professional, Material Seller). Duplicate registrations are rejected globally.
  • Cryptographic OTP Security: Verification codes are 6 numeric digits generated using secure random algorithms, hashed server-side, and expire in 10 minutes.
  • Brute-Force & Spam Protection: Maximum 5 verification attempts are allowed per code, enforced by a 60-second cooldown timer between resends.

Session & Token Security

BuildBid uses stateless cryptographic JWT authentication backed by server-side security timestamps.

Server-Side Invalidation

When you change your password or click revoke, your account's security timestamp is immediately updated server-side. All tokens issued prior to that moment are rejected on every device.

Stateless Cryptography & Zero Plaintext

No plaintext passwords or raw tokens are stored on the server. Password credentials are authenticated using salted BCrypt hashing.

Revoke All Active Sessions

Lost a device or want to ensure no other browser has access? This will immediately invalidate all active tokens and require you to log in again with your credentials.

Your Privacy & Data Ownership

You have full ownership of your data on BuildBid. You can export a snapshot of all personal, project, requirement, and order information associated with your account at any time. The exported file is securely generated directly from our authoritative database in JSON format.

What is Included in Your Export

  • Account Details: Name, username, email, phone, and registration timestamp.
  • Profile Information: Addresses, contact details, about section, and language preference.
  • Verification Status: Verification status and timestamps for email and phone.
  • Construction Preferences: Preferred project types, property sizes, quality tiers, and budget ranges.
  • Saved Locations: All registered site addresses and default delivery markers.
  • Regional Preferences: Time zone, currency, measurement units, and date/number formats.
  • My Projects: Details, budgets, scopes, and specifications of your posted projects.
  • Requests & Material Orders: Material requirements, service requests, and confirmed orders.
  • Hired Professionals: Accepted direct hires and trade professionals on your projects.

What is Never Exported (For Your Security)

  • Password Hashes & Passwords: Your credentials remain strictly encrypted on our secure server.
  • Active Session Tokens: JWT access tokens and session authorization secrets are never included.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs): OTP codes, hashes, and temporary verification tokens are omitted.
  • Private Information of Other Users: Contractor internal records and other customer details are strictly isolated.
  • Payment Credentials: BuildBid never stores or exports raw credit card or banking secrets.

Deactivate Account

Temporary / Reversible

Temporarily pause your BuildBid customer account. While deactivated:

  • You will be logged out of all active sessions immediately.
  • Direct login will be blocked until your account is reactivated.
  • Your personal profile, addresses, and preferences remain securely preserved.
  • No personal or business data is deleted during deactivation.

Permanently Delete Account

Irreversible / Destructive

Permanently erase your BuildBid customer profile and personal records. This action cannot be undone:

  • Your customer profile, saved locations, and regional preferences are permanently erased.
  • Your password, security records, and active authentication tokens are invalidated.
  • Shared legal and transactional records (orders, bids) are retained for audit and anonymized.
  • Requires password verification & 6-digit OTP delivered to both verified email and mobile.

Security Notice & Prerequisite Policy

For your protection against unauthorized destruction of customer accounts, permanent deletion requires having both a verified email address and a verified mobile phone number. If either channel is unverified, please visit the Email & Mobile Verification (Step 2) tab to verify them first before initiating deletion.
